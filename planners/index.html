What is on it

Weeks run Monday to Sunday under Japanese day kanji, with ISO week numbers and the two-week sprint they fall in. Danish public holidays and weekends are striped; Danish tax and property deadlines are marked. Eclipses, meteor showers, solstices and supermoons visible from Denmark are drawn in, along with the average Aalborg temperature for each day and a quote at the head of each month.

Calendar files come from any calendar app's export — in Google Calendar, Settings → Import & export. Event times are shown in Copenhagen time.

GlyphClock time

The day planners replace clock hours with GlyphClock blocks: sixteen glyphs of 90 minutes each, counted from UTC midnight. Each row shows the local time the glyph starts in the zone you pick. Because most offsets are not a multiple of 90 minutes, the glyphs shift when daylight saving time starts or ends — pick the date you will use the planner on.
